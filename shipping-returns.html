Return Policy

We accept returns within 7 days of delivery for unused items in original packaging. Perishable items (Dry Fruits, Fruits) are non-returnable unless damaged on arrival.

To initiate a return, please contact our team at Sea2sky1@hotmail.com with your order number and reason for return.

Refunds

Once your return is received and inspected, refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to your original payment method.
